# Pavona (OpenTitan-family) sweep — per-IP results

[Pavona](https://github.com/pavona/pavona) is the OpenTitan-derived SoC family (OpenTitan-hardened Ibex core, the
TL-UL fabric and every peripheral / crypto IP, and the two full chips
`top_egret` and `top_dragonfly`).  Each IP family below is imported **verbatim**
from the Pavona RTL into its own `test/pavona_*_equiv/` directory and swept by
the nightly [Sweep pavona](https://github.com/alainmarcel/uhdm2rtlil/actions/workflows/sweep-pavona.yml)
workflow (`test/core_sweep.py`), which publishes the per-module table to the run's
step summary: the `read_slang` netlist's own Verilator co-sim vs the RTL (slang
baseline), formal equivalence read_uhdm vs `read_slang` (SAT miter from reset),
the structural opt-check (dropped-driver detection), and the read_uhdm Verilator
co-sim pass / divergence count.  Every divergence is adjudicated by the SAT miter
(see the [Verification Methodology](../README.md#verification-methodology)).

## Full chips

Whole-chip check (`test/pavona_chips/`, [README](../test/pavona_chips/README.md)):
the complete chip is elaborated by `read_uhdm` and `read_slang`, every DIRECT
instance of the top is SAT-mitered from reset and Verilator co-simulated against
its RTL with the instance's parameters, and the whole chip is co-simulated
(RTL vs read_uhdm netlist vs read_slang netlist).

| Chip | Direct instances | Result | Nightly |
|------|------------------|--------|---------|
| **top_egret** | **47** | **47 / 47 instances formally proven** vs read_slang; full-chip Verilator co-sim **PASS** (2001 cycles); per-instance co-sim nightly (found the pinmux `'{pull_en: 1'b1, default: '0}` power-on attribute bug and the `pinmux_strap_sampling` conditional-arm pattern bug) | [Sweep pavona](https://github.com/alainmarcel/uhdm2rtlil/actions/workflows/sweep-pavona.yml) |
| **top_dragonfly** | **51** | **51 / 51 instances formally proven** vs read_slang; full-chip Verilator co-sim **PASS** (2001 cycles); per-instance co-sim: no read_uhdm-only divergence (rows marked `⚠ shared div` differ from the RTL identically for read_uhdm and read_slang — a testbench parameter limitation, not a frontend difference) | [Sweep pavona](https://github.com/alainmarcel/uhdm2rtlil/actions/workflows/sweep-pavona.yml) |
